About this event

The Energy God Tour was one of Lagos's most electrifying nights of 2025. Timaya brought his signature high-energy performance to the Eko Hotel Grand Ballroom, delivering an unforgettable 3-hour set packed with his biggest hits — from Dem Mama to Chulo Vibes. The crowd was sold out weeks in advance, with fans travelling from Abuja, Port Harcourt and beyond.

Special guest appearances from Patoranking and Small Doctor sent the crowd into a frenzy. The production was a step above anything seen on the Lagos circuit this year — pyrotechnics, live band, and a light show that had everyone talking.
